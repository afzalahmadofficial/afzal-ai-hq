# Revenue Workflow

**Version:** 1.0  
**Project:** Afzal Ahmad AI HQ  
**Workflow:** Services → Offers → Leads → Proposals → Clients → Revenue Tracking → Retention → Referrals  
**Status:** Approved  
**Priority:** High

---

# 1. Objective

Create a structured business workflow that converts qualified demand into legitimate service revenue while protecting trust, quality, and long-term client relationships.

AI HQ supports research, organization, preparation, and analytics. Human approval remains required for important commercial commitments.

---

# 2. Core Principle

```text
Market Need
   ↓
Offer
   ↓
Qualified Lead
   ↓
Discovery
   ↓
Proposal
   ↓
Agreement
   ↓
Delivery
   ↓
Invoice / Payment
   ↓
Client Success
   ↓
Retention / Referral
```

Revenue should come from genuine value delivered to clients, not misleading claims or aggressive outreach.

---

# 3. Offer Definition

Each service offer should clearly define:

- Target client
- Problem solved
- Deliverables
- Scope
- Expected process
- Timeline
- Pricing model
- Exclusions
- Success criteria

Do not promise outcomes that cannot reasonably be supported.

---

# 4. Potential Service Areas

The current business strategy may include services such as:

- SEO content strategy
- SEO content writing
- GEO optimization
- AI-assisted content systems
- Content research
- YouTube research and strategy
- AI automation consulting
- Personal-brand content systems

The active project specification is the source of truth for which offers are currently active.

---

# 5. Lead Sources

Revenue opportunities may originate from:

- Organic content
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Website/SEO
- Referrals
- Existing relationships
- Direct prospecting
- Market intelligence

All acquisition activity should follow `CLIENT_ACQUISITION_WORKFLOW.md`.

---

# 6. Lead Qualification

Before investing significant proposal effort, assess:

- Client fit
- Problem clarity
- Service fit
- Budget suitability
- Timeline
- Decision authority
- Ability to deliver successfully

A lead can be rejected when the project is clearly unsuitable.

---

# 7. Discovery

A discovery process should establish:

- Current situation
- Business objective
- Main problem
- Desired outcome
- Existing resources
- Constraints
- Timeline
- Decision process

AI may help prepare questions and summarize approved information, but should not fabricate client requirements.

---

# 8. Proposal

A proposal may contain:

- Client problem
- Recommended solution
- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Responsibilities
- Pricing
- Assumptions
- Revision policy
- Next steps

Human review is required before sending a binding commercial proposal.

---

# 9. Commercial Approval

The human owner must approve important:

- Pricing
- Discounts
- Scope changes
- Contract terms
- Refund commitments
- Guarantees
- Payment terms

AI must not independently make binding financial commitments unless explicitly authorized within a controlled system.

---

# 10. Client Conversion

A lead becomes a client only after the required commercial agreement and onboarding conditions are completed.

Suggested states:

- `LEAD`
- `QUALIFIED`
- `DISCOVERY`
- `PROPOSAL`
- `NEGOTIATION`
- `WON`
- `LOST`
- `ONBOARDING`
- `ACTIVE`
- `COMPLETED`
- `RETAINED`

---

# 11. Revenue Tracking

Track, where appropriate:

- Lead source
- Offer
- Proposal value
- Won value
- Payment status
- Revenue date
- Delivery status
- Client lifetime value
- Referral source

Financial records should be stored in an appropriate secure system rather than exposed in ordinary AI memory.

---

# 12. Delivery Handoff

After conversion:

```text
Won Client
   ↓
Onboarding
   ↓
Scope Confirmation
   ↓
Production
   ↓
QA
   ↓
Delivery
   ↓
Client Feedback
```

Delivery should remain aligned with the agreed scope.

---

# 13. Client Success

Measure success against agreed deliverables and appropriate outcomes.

Do not redefine success after delivery simply to make a project appear successful.

Capture:

- Deliverables completed
- Client feedback
- Issues
- Improvements
- Results when measurable
- Follow-up opportunities

---

# 14. Retention

Retention opportunities may include:

- Recurring content services
- Monthly SEO/GEO support
- YouTube strategy support
- AI workflow maintenance
- Analytics and optimization

Retention should be based on continuing client value, not artificial dependency.

---

# 15. Referrals

Satisfied clients may be invited to provide referrals when appropriate.

Referral requests should be respectful and should never pressure a client.

Track referral source when authorized and useful.

---

# 16. Revenue Analytics

Review metrics such as:

- Qualified leads
- Proposal rate
- Win rate
- Average deal value
- Revenue by offer
- Revenue by acquisition source
- Delivery margin/effort where measurable
- Retention rate
- Referral rate

Avoid optimizing for revenue at the expense of client fit or service quality.

---

# 17. Intelligence Integration

Daily Intelligence may identify:

- New market demand
- Emerging service needs
- Competitor positioning
- Industry changes
- New content opportunities
- Potential client segments

These signals should feed the decision-making and offer-development workflows rather than automatically trigger commercial actions.

---

# 18. Pricing Changes

Pricing changes should follow change-management and human approval procedures.

Before changing pricing, evaluate:

- Market evidence
- Delivery cost/effort
- Positioning
- Client value
- Existing commitments

Historical pricing decisions should remain traceable.

---

# 19. Failure Handling

If a proposal, payment, or client-management integration fails:

1. Preserve the current state.
2. Record the failure.
3. Avoid duplicate actions.
4. Verify the commercial status manually when needed.
5. Resume only after the state is confirmed.

Never claim payment or conversion without reliable confirmation.

---

# 20. Privacy & Security

Protect client information and financial data.

Never store passwords, API keys, payment credentials, or private authentication information in ordinary workflow records or AI prompts.

Follow `SECURITY_WORKFLOW.md` for security controls.

---

# 21. Learning Loop

```text
Lead
 ↓
Proposal
 ↓
Client Outcome
 ↓
Analytics
 ↓
Learning
 ↓
Better Offer + Better Qualification
```

Feed useful lessons into the decision, analytics, and knowledge-memory workflows.

---

# 22. Success Criteria

The revenue workflow succeeds when AI HQ can consistently identify qualified demand, present clear value-based offers, support accurate proposals, track commercial states, protect client trust, measure revenue outcomes, and improve acquisition, delivery, retention, and referrals through evidence-based learning.
