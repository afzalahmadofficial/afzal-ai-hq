# Content Calendar Workflow

**Version:** 1.0  
**Project:** Afzal Ahmad AI HQ  
**Workflow:** Intelligence → Ideas → Prioritization → Calendar → Production → Approval → Publishing → Analytics  
**Status:** Approved  
**Priority:** High

## 1. Objective

Turn validated intelligence and content opportunities into a coordinated, realistic publishing calendar across LinkedIn, YouTube, Blog, Shorts/Reels, and other approved channels.

The calendar is a planning layer, not a guarantee that every idea must be published.

## 2. Core Principle

```text
Intelligence
   ↓
Content Opportunities
   ↓
Prioritize
   ↓
Calendar
   ↓
Produce
   ↓
QA + Approval
   ↓
Publish
   ↓
Measure
   ↓
Optimize
```

## 3. Calendar Inputs

Use validated inputs from:

- Daily Intelligence Report
- SEO research
- GEO research
- YouTube research
- Competitor analysis
- Audience questions
- Previous content performance
- Business priorities
- Product/service priorities

## 4. Content Record

Each calendar item should include, where relevant:

- Content ID
- Working title
- Topic
- Content pillar
- Audience
- Platform
- Format
- Goal
- Source/evidence
- Priority
- Planned date/time
- Status
- Related content
- Approval status

## 5. Content Pillars

The active brand strategy defines the official content pillars.

Every major calendar item should map to at least one approved pillar unless it is deliberately classified as an experiment.

## 6. Platform Mapping

Typical mapping:

| Platform | Suitable Formats |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Posts, carousels, insights, personal-brand lessons |
| YouTube | Long-form videos, explainers, research-driven videos |
| Blog | SEO/GEO articles, guides, research content |
| Shorts/Reels | Short educational clips and repurposed insights |

The same topic may be adapted differently for each platform rather than copied mechanically.

## 7. Prioritization

Prioritize using factors such as:

- Audience relevance
- Strategic relevance
- Evidence strength
- Timeliness
- Search/discovery opportunity
- Business value
- Production effort
- Existing content performance

High priority does not automatically mean immediate publication.

## 8. Publishing Cadence

The calendar should balance consistency with realistic production capacity.

Do not create a schedule that requires more output than the available workflow can reliably produce.

## 9. Scheduling

Scheduled entries must include the correct timezone.

For the current AI HQ operating plan, the **Daily Intelligence Report is scheduled for 2:00 PM PKT** and can feed new opportunities into the next planning cycle.

Publishing schedules should be managed through `SCHEDULING_WORKFLOW.md`.

## 10. Statuses

Recommended states:

- `IDEA`
- `RESEARCHING`
- `PLANNED`
- `IN_PRODUCTION`
- `QA`
- `READY_FOR_APPROVAL`
- `APPROVED`
- `SCHEDULED`
- `PUBLISHED`
- `CANCELLED`
- `REPURPOSED`

## 11. Production Handoff

When an item reaches `PLANNED`, create the required production task.

```text
Calendar Item
    ↓
Research Brief
    ↓
Draft / Script
    ↓
Assets
    ↓
QA
    ↓
Approval
```

Use `CONTENT_PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md` for production details.

## 12. Repurposing

A strong source asset may create multiple approved derivatives.

Example:

```text
YouTube Research / Video
       ↓
LinkedIn Post
       ↓
Short Clip
       ↓
Blog Article
       ↓
GEO/SEO Supporting Assets
```

Use `CONTENT_REPURPOSING_WORKFLOW.md` rather than creating disconnected copies.

## 13. Content Balance

The calendar should balance:

- Educational content
- Authority-building content
- Practical how-to content
- Research/intelligence content
- Audience engagement
- Service/business content
- Experiments

Avoid turning the entire calendar into direct promotion.

## 14. Approval

External publication follows `PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md`.

If a calendar item changes substantially after approval, it should return to the appropriate QA/approval stage.

## 15. Analytics Feedback

After publication, connect the item to analytics.

Review, where available:

- Impressions/views
- Engagement
- Clicks
- Watch time/retention
- Search performance
- Leads/conversions
- Audience feedback

Performance should influence future calendar priorities.

## 16. Calendar Review

Conduct periodic reviews to identify:

- Overdue items
- Repeated topics
- Weak content patterns
- High-performing themes
- Gaps in content pillars
- Production bottlenecks
- Opportunities for repurposing

## 17. Cancellation Rules

An item may be cancelled or replaced when:

- Evidence becomes outdated
- The topic is no longer strategically relevant
- Better intelligence appears
- Required information cannot be verified
- Production capacity is unavailable
- The topic creates unnecessary risk

Cancellation is preferable to publishing low-quality or misleading content merely to maintain a schedule.

## 18. Failure Handling

If a scheduled item cannot be produced or published:

1. Preserve the calendar record.
2. Mark the correct failure/status.
3. Identify the cause.
4. Reschedule only when appropriate.
5. Avoid duplicate publication.
6. Escalate important failures through the incident/error workflows.

## 19. Calendar Memory

Keep historical calendar records so the system can understand:

- What was published
- When it was published
- Which platform was used
- Which topics were repeated
- Which content performed well
- Which ideas were cancelled

Do not store secrets or unnecessary personal information in calendar records.

## 20. Success Criteria

The workflow succeeds when AI HQ can continuously convert validated intelligence into a realistic cross-platform content plan, coordinate production and approvals, publish consistently, avoid duplicate or low-quality output, and improve future planning using actual performance data.
